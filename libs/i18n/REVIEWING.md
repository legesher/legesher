# Reviewing a language pack

The reviewing guide lives in [legesher/language-packs](https://github.com/legesher/language-packs),
the contribution home for Legesher's language packs.

Reviews arrive there as pull requests. Copy your language's template, `reference/<tag>.md`, to
`reviews/<tag>/<tag>-<your-github-username>.md`, work through the terms, tick the Required boxes,
and open a pull request. A maintainer replies within 7 days.

- [Start here](https://github.com/legesher/language-packs#start-here)
- [The full reviewing guide](https://github.com/legesher/language-packs/blob/main/REVIEWING.md)

More ways to contribute are coming.
