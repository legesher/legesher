## How to Get Added

Contributors are recognized automatically when their pull request is merged.
You can also be added for non-code contributions (documentation, translation
reviews, bug reports, etc.) — just ask in your issue or PR!

We use the [all-contributors](https://allcontributors.org/) specification.
Contributions of all kinds are welcome.
